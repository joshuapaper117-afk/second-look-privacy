Support

Second Look · Help and contact

Second Look is a personal project built and run by a single independent developer. If something is not working, or you have a question, email thesecondlookapp@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a real person, usually within a few days.

Common questions

How does Second Look work?
Add something you are thinking of buying, with a name, a price and a category. It starts a cooling-off wait, 30 days by default. When the wait ends, mark it Bought or Skip.

Can I change the 30 days?
Yes. When you add or edit an item, pick any target date you like.

I did not get a reminder.
Reminders arrive at 9:00 am on the day an item is ready. Check that notifications are allowed for Second Look in the iPhone Settings app, under Notifications.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in page, choose the forgot password link and follow the steps. If that does not work, email us from the address you signed up with.

How do I change the currency?
Tap your avatar in the top left, then Currency management. You can choose pounds, dollars, euros or your own symbol.

How do I delete my account?
Tap your avatar in the top left, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything saved in it, immediately.

Who can see my items?
Only you. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

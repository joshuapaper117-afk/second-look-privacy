Privacy Policy

Second Look · Last updated 3 October 2026

Second Look is a personal project built and run by a single independent developer, not a company. This page explains, in plain language, what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

Creating an account requires an email address, handled by Amazon Cognito (our authentication provider) to sign you in and keep your data tied to your account.

Everything else is content you type in yourself, meaning the things you're thinking about buying: an item name, price, category, the date you added it, and the date you've chosen to revisit it. We don't collect anything beyond what you enter.

We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. We don't access your contacts, location, camera, or photos. We don't know anything about you beyond your email and whatever you've typed into the app.

How it's used

Your data exists for one purpose: to show your list back to you, synced across your own devices. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't look at it.

Where it's stored

Your account and data are stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS), under our own AWS account. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or processed by any other company or third party.

A local copy is also kept on your device so the app works offline, and is cleared if you sign out or delete your account.

Notifications

Reminders ("this item is ready for a second look") are scheduled entirely on your device through Apple's own notification system. No server or third party is involved in sending them, and nothing about them leaves your phone.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (your avatar in the top left, then Delete account). This permanently removes your account and everything in it, including your email and your saved items, immediately, with no recovery period.

Signing out, rather than deleting, simply signs you out; your data stays in your account for next time.

Children's privacy

Second Look isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since this is a small, actively developed personal project, we'd encourage checking back occasionally rather than expecting a notification.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or a request about your data: thesecondlookapp@gmail.com.
